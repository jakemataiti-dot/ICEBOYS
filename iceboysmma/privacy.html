Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Ice Boys MMA ("we", "us") runs a martial arts gym at 64 Cambridge Terrace, Te Aro, Wellington. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what we do with it. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you enquire about training, book a trial, or sign up, we may collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your answers to our form questions, such as which class or morning you'd like to start
  • notes from our conversations with you about booking your trial or membership
  • your membership and payment status

We collect this through our website forms and through lead forms on Facebook and Instagram. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card details.

How we use it

  • to contact you about your enquiry, trial or membership
  • to book and remind you about classes
  • to manage your membership and payments
  • to keep our training sessions safe

We don't sell your information or give it to anyone for their own marketing.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored using third-party services we rely on to run the gym, including Google (Google Workspace), Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Telegram (for messages between our staff) and Stripe (payments). Some of these services may store data outside New Zealand. Only our coaching and admin team can access it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're enquiring or training with us, and for as long as we reasonably need it afterwards for our records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond as soon as we can. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
