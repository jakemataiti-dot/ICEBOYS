Who We Are

Our Story

Ice Boys MMA was founded out of necessity — when there's nowhere that facilitates your goals in a way you see fit, you have to be able to trust your own judgement. After meeting at another local gym, for over a year we visited other gyms, trained in public spaces, sometimes even outside.

In our journey we found thinking critically in your own best interest always pays off, even if you have to do it alone.

We started Ice Boys MMA so we could share our passion for combat sport, and continue to develop our own coaching methodology.

Ice Boys MMA is Wellington's first and only dedicated Mixed Martial Arts Gym. We are working hard to build a strong, core fight team. Our aim is to help facilitate the growth of combat skills across all disciplines.

We have a firm focus on skill acquisition. Our goal as coaching staff is to increase both the individual and collective skill level as much as possible every session. And to create adaptable, confident individuals, based on real proof in training. We strive to do so in as safe a manner as possible, so you can focus on getting better instead of getting hurt.

We train beginners, all the way through to professional fighters. We have experience coaching all levels, so no matter where you're at, we can meet you.

If you've had the desire to pick up the skills training MMA has to offer, to build confidence or fight. You're keen to train with people that are on the same wave, AND you're willing to put the work in and think for yourself — you've found your place.

The Team

Coaches

Jacob Mataiti

Founder & Head Coach

An amateur fighter of 9 fights turned coach, Jacob draws on his competitive experience to aid his perspective coaching - initially to help his friends pursue their own professional aspirations. Backed by a representative background across multiple sports and 8 years of combat sport experience, he brings a deep passion for the technical intricacies of martial arts, and excelling human movement and performance in the field. As well as what honing those skills can do for your personal development. Jacob strives to create an environment where developing those skills is not a rarity but the expectation and the norm.

Feargus Gordon

Coach & Competitor

A combat sports athlete with experience across a range of disciplines, including Muay Thai, kickboxing, boxing, MMA, freestyle wrestling and BJJ. He currently has a combined record of 12-2, with 7 of those wins coming by way of finish, and holds a purple belt in BJJ.

Feargus is passionate about all things related to martial arts, not just as a competitor but also as a coach. He works in the education sector and has a passion for helping others with their learning. He uses this experience to guide his coaching in a way that is clear, accessible and enjoyable.

Sanjay Patel

Coach

Inspired to start training after watching Adesanya's win over Whittaker in 2019, Sanjay quickly fell in love with the challenge, discipline, and technical side of combat sports. As he continues to play multiple sports, he brings a rounded approach to coaching and training. Holding a Bachelor of Sport & Exercise majoring in Coaching and Management, he enjoys seeing people develop both on and off the mats — whilst still being able to have a laugh.
