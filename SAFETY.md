# Ice Boys MMA — Safety Standards

Last updated: 2 Nov 2026

Our approach is preventative. The goal is zero concussions in the gym.

## Force and head injuries

- Coaches understand brain injury in combat sports and train with it in mind.
- Expectations on force are set clearly. Anyone who goes over that level is pulled up straight away.
- One concussion since opening in January 2026.

## Concussion protocol

- Minimum 3-week stand-down after any concussion.
- Return after that is at the coach's discretion, and errs heavily on the side of caution.

## Sparring

- Regular sparring is exploratory: learning to move safely and engage. The aim is never to hurt a partner, only to build skill.
- Hard sparring is for fighters only, and only when preparing for a competition.
- Ground and pound to a stoppage is never done to a training partner. It is only drilled on pads and bags in conditioning.

## Fighters and competition

- All three coaches must agree a person is ready to fight before they are matched.

## Headgear

- Not needed for regular sparring. Used for hard sparring, where there is a risk of cuts.
- Optional for anyone who prefers it.

## Under-18s

- Can spar with written consent from a parent or guardian.
- Light, technical sparring only. Never hard sparring.
- The same 3-week concussion stand-down applies, and parents are told the same day.
